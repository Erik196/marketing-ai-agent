# StudySprint — fictional demo brand guide

This guide defines an invented brand for a learning demonstration, not a real customer's approved policy.

## Voice and tone

Use a friendly, calm, encouraging voice. Prefer plain language and short sentences. Acknowledge that university life is busy without shaming students for procrastination. Emphasize manageable study routines rather than relentless productivity.

## Claims and calls to action

Avoid guarantees about grades, mental health, or time savings. Do not invent endorsements or discounts. Suggested draft calls to action include “Plan your next study session” and “Build a study routine that works for you.” A signup URL is unknown and should be a placeholder.
